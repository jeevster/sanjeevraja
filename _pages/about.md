I'm a fourth-year Computer Science PhD student at Berkeley AI Research [(BAIR)](https://bair.berkeley.edu/){:target="\_blank"} advised by [Aditi Krishnapriyan](https://a1k12.github.io/){:target="\_blank"}. 

My interests lie at the intersection of machine learning and atomistic modeling, with a particular focus on developing ML methods to accelerate [molecular dynamics simulations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Molecular_dynamics){:target="\_blank"}. Recently, I have also been working on generative modeling of molecular conformational ensembles.

In the past, I worked on ML for accelerating climate simulations. I worked with [Jaideep Pathak](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=cevw0gkAAAAJ&hl=en){:target="\_blank"} and [Anima Anandkumar](http://tensorlab.cms.caltech.edu/users/anima/){:target="\_blank"} on [FourCastNet](https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.11214){:target="\_blank"}, which was a state-of-the-art weather prediction model at the time.

I love [Carnatic music](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carnatic_music){:target="\_blank"}, and I am a semi-professional [mridangam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mridangam){:target="\_blank"} player. I developed a state-of-the-art [Carnatic raga classifier](https://huggingface.co/spaces/jeevster/carnatic-raga-classifier){:target="\_blank"} capable of identifying 150 common Carnatic scales. I also enjoy tennis, and particularly Roger Federer. Unfortunately, however, no amount of machine learning will reverse the result of the 2019 Wimbledon Final.
